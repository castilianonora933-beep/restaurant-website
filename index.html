Our story

The Royal Plate opened in 2012 as a single dining room with a wood-fired tandoor and a simple promise: treat every guest like royalty. Today the same family recipes sit beside modern techniques, served in a room lit by candlelight and brass.

Our food philosophy

Cook slowly, season honestly, source locally. Our spices are ground in-house each week, our dairy comes from nearby farms, and nothing leaves the kitchen that we would not serve at our own table.
